Welcome to the Egg Menu: Boarding House Edition!

I know eating on a college budget can easily get repetitive and boring, but upgrading your meals doesn't have to be expensive. Wondering how far 200 pesos can go? You're in the right page! Here are fun and budget-friendly egg recipes you must try.

Instead of plain Sunny Side Up Eggs
Try Shakshuka Recipe

Ingredients:

  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tomato
  • 2 cloves onions
  • 2 diced red bell pepper
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • 1/4 teaspoon tomato paste
  • 1/4 teaspoon paprika or chili paste
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Fresh parsley for garnish (optional)

Instead of plain Hard Boiled Eggs
Try Egg Sisig Recipe

Ingredients:

  • 4 hard-boiled eggs
  • 1/2 cup diced onions
  • 1/2 cup diced bell peppers
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/4 cup oyster sauce
  • 1 tablespoon soy sauce
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Chopped green onions for garnish (optional)

Instead of plain Scrambled Eggs
Try Egg Drop Soup Recipe

Ingredients:

  • 2 eggs
  • 4 cups chicken broth
  • 1 chicken cube
  • 1/2 teaspoon grated ginger
  • cornstarch for thickening (optional)
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • sesame oil (optional)
